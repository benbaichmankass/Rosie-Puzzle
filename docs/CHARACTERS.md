# Cast and places

Draw each character in your game's own style but keep these traits. Colours: `src/shared/characters.ts`.

## People
- **Bentzi** ("Ben" in the spec): stocky, light skin, short dark-brown hair, full brown beard, sunglasses
  pushed up on his head, black t-shirt. Big expressive grin.
- **Shai** ("Chai" in the spec; the game uses Shai): slimmer, olive skin, very short dark hair, trimmed dark
  beard, wide smile, sand/beige t-shirt, sunglasses up on his head.
- Names in game text: **Bentzi**, **Shai**, **Rosie**.

## Rosie (the dog)
Lean medium-size dog with thin legs and a long thin tail. White coat with light grey freckles/ticking.
Black head and ears with a white stripe down the face and a white muzzle. A big black spot on her rump
near the tail. Wears a teal harness on walks (red collar in the bed puzzle). Curious, sniffs everything,
sleeps curled on the couch or the bed.

## Bunnies (three, they live in the yard)
1. White rabbit, pink ears, red eyes (the biggest).
2. Black-and-white "Dutch" rabbit: black head, ears and rear, white face stripe and white band round the middle.
3. Grey-and-white rabbit: grey head, ears and back patches, white face stripe and chest.
They hop around the dirt yard, nibble plants and huddle together.

## Turtles (two)
Red-eared sliders: dark olive shells, green skin with yellow stripes and a red patch behind the eye.
They live in a small square sunken pond in the yard's corner and sun themselves on a flat stone, often stacked.

## The yard
Packed brown dirt with dry leaves and gravel. High cream/beige rendered walls. A tree in the corner with a
metal colander hanging on it. A teal garden hose snaking across the ground. Low green wire fencing around
plant beds. A big wooden cable-spool used as a table, wooden pallet planters along a fence, a green
watering can, potted herbs. The sunken turtle pond in the corner.

## The living room
White walls, a dark grey L-shaped sofa with cushions, a glass coffee table, a wall shelf with books, a wall
lamp, an AC unit above the window, a door to the yard. Rosie often sleeps on the sofa.

## Jerusalem Boulevard, Yafo
Wide boulevard with tall ficus and palm trees, sandy park strips with wooden benches and picnic tables, the
red-line light rail running along it, bike riders and pedestrians, low Jaffa apartment blocks.

## The house plan
See `docs/map-sketch.jpg` (Ben's sketch) and `src/map/layout.ts`. The yard wraps the house on the left and
bottom; the street door is on the yard's left wall; the bunny/garden wall is the yard's left wall lower down;
the hose-shooter wall is the bottom wall; the living room's left wall has a window, the front door to the
yard, and a window with an AC unit; the bedroom is below the living room; the kitchen and the bathroom are a
row along the top right, the kitchen attached to the living room.
